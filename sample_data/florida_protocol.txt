FLORIDA DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION
Bureau of Exceptional Education and Student Services

IDEA PART B MONITORING PROTOCOL
2024-2025 School Year

INDICATOR 1: CHILD FIND AND EVALUATION TIMELINES

Requirement: Initial evaluations and reevaluations are conducted within required 
timelines as required by 34 CFR §300.301(c)(1). Districts must complete initial 
evaluations within 60 calendar days of receiving parental consent.

Required Evidence:
- Parent consent forms with dates
- Completed evaluation reports with completion dates
- Timeline tracking documentation
- Child Find procedures documentation

INDICATOR 2: IEP DEVELOPMENT AND CONTENT

Requirement: IEPs are developed with required components and parent participation 
as required by 34 CFR §300.320-324. IEPs must include present levels of performance, 
measurable annual goals, special education services, and participation in state assessments.

Required Evidence:
- Completed IEPs with all required components
- IEP meeting notices to parents
- Parent attendance/participation documentation
- Progress monitoring data

INDICATOR 3: FREE APPROPRIATE PUBLIC EDUCATION (FAPE)

Requirement: Students with disabilities receive a Free Appropriate Public Education 
in the Least Restrictive Environment (LRE) as required by 34 CFR §300.114-120.

Required Evidence:
- IEP documentation of LRE considerations
- Service delivery logs
- Inclusion data and documentation
- Continuum of placement options documentation

INDICATOR 4: PROCEDURAL SAFEGUARDS

Requirement: Districts provide procedural safeguards to parents including Prior 
Written Notice (PWN) and procedural safeguards notice as required by 34 CFR §300.503-504.

Required Evidence:
- Prior Written Notice documentation
- Procedural Safeguards Notice provided to parents
- Parent consent forms
- Records of parent communication
